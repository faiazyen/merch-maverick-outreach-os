## Differences vs. EU (strategy adjustments)

1. **Event-driven > venue-driven.** EU signal was venue openings; US signal is *race registrations*.
   Lead with the deadline: "your kit on the start line in Vegas, or it's free."
2. **Premium positioning works harder.** Reserve/Padel Haus are luxury plays — US padel outreach
   should sell retail-grade quality, not price.
3. **Chains matter more.** Bouldering Project, solidcore, Ace Pickleball, Pickleball Kingdom —
   the US is a franchise economy. One HQ yes = 10-1,200 locations.
4. **Air freight is a bigger differentiator.** US decorators quote 4-8 weeks; we quote a written
   date. Same offer, sharper edge.
